# Changelog

## v1.1.0

**Added**
- Expanded default word bank from ~50 to ~150 words, covering greetings, question words, pronouns, family terms, numbers, time, colors, common verbs, places, food, adjectives, weather, money/shopping terms, and everyday particles (`gyud`, `lang`, `pud`, etc.)

## v1.0.0

**Added**
- Main menu with Play, Word bank, and Settings screens
- Editable word bank — add or remove Cebuano/English word pairs directly in the app, no code editing required
- Reset-to-default option for the word bank
- Settings screen: adjustable question count per round (5–30) and an optional per-question countdown timer (5–30 seconds) with a visual progress bar
- Score, streak, and best-score tracking, persisted in the browser via `localStorage`

## v0.1.0

**Added**
- Initial single-file quiz: multiple-choice Cebuano-to-English drilling with a fixed ~50-word bank, 10 questions per round, score tracking
